📋 Recovery Triage Guide

Missing W-2 forms? Use this recovery triage matrix to find equivalent basis records:

Level 1
Wage Differential: Compare Wages from NJ-1040 Line 15 (or PA-40 Line 1) with Federal 1040 Line 1. The difference represents state-taxed pre-tax deferrals (Box 12 codes).
Level 2
Medicare Wage Proxy: Pull IRS Wage/Income Transcripts. Calculate Medicare Wages (Box 5) - Federal wages (Box 1). Medicare wages serve as a reliable state wage proxy.
Level 3
Custodian Request: Query the broker/custodian for a "Participant Contribution Ledger" to isolate lifetime employee pre-tax deferrals.

Apply State Taxation Engine:
New Jersey Pro-Rata Model (Worksheet C): Withdrawals are tax-free in the ratio of your unrecovered state basis to the total value of all IRAs. Under-reporting basis triggers double taxation.
Pennsylvania Cost Recovery Model: Employee deferrals are taxed upon contribution. Qualified distributions after age 59½ are 100% tax-free. Non-qualified early distributions recover basis first (100% tax-free FIFO) until basis is depleted.
Massachusetts Pro-Rata Model: Traditional IRA contributions are non-deductible (creating basis). Withdrawals recover basis pro-rata based on the ratio of MA basis to account value.
